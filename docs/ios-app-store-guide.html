    01

    Clear the blockers first

    These will get the app rejected or stop review from starting.

    Blocker

    Reviewer sign-in. The app signs in with phone + OTP only. Apple's reviewer can't receive your SMS, so they can't reach checkout, orders or account deletion. Set up a test phone number on the production backend that always accepts a fixed code, and put both in the review notes (step 09).

    Blocker

    Account deletion must really work (guideline 5.1.1(v)). Profile → Delete account calls DELETE /store/customers/me. Confirm production implements it. The website's /delete-account form isn't wired to anything yet (rukn_flutter/RELEASE.md).

    Blocker

    Age rating questions. Since 31 January 2026 App Store Connect won't let you submit until the updated age-rating questionnaire is answered (in-app controls, capabilities, medical/wellness, violent themes). Answers are in step 04.

    Ready

    Screenshots and header art are made. 48 screenshots plus two header images at Apple's exact sizes are in store-assets/appstore/. See step 07.

    Check

    Seller name. On an Individual developer account the App Store shows the person's name as the seller, not the company. To show "Al Rukn Al Thamin Gold & Jewelry", the account must be an Organization (needs a D-U-N-S number).

    06

    Accessibility Nutrition Labels

    Your app → General → App Accessibility. optional

    Not required to submit. Each claim (VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, Dark Interface, Differentiate Without Color, Sufficient Contrast, Reduced Motion, Captions, Audio Descriptions) means the app supports it across its common tasks, and users can report wrong claims.

    Recommend

    Skip this for the first release. Fill it only after testing VoiceOver and Larger Text on sign-in, browsing, checkout and the invest screens. An empty label shows "Not provided"; a wrong one invites complaints.
